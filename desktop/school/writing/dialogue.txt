Soon after I sat at the old diner, a young waitress approached me and asked me for my order, saying: "Hello,
my name is Lindsey. What can I get for you today?" "Can I get a large lemonade, a large fry, and a
cheeseburger, please?", I replied. "Sure, it'll be right out.", she said before she smiled and walked away.
When she returned with my order, she said: "Here you go!" and placed it on the table; however, a man who was
leaving bumped into her, causing her to drop the lemonade. "I'm so sorry, let me clean this for you." Being
polite, I said "Don't worry about it, it's fine", grabbed some napkins, and helped her clean the spill. We
cleaned it up, and she said "I'll be right back". She returned with another lemonade and gave it to me.
Before she moved to the next table, she said "Sorry about that, hope you enjoy your meal!", and pushed her
hair over her left ear and laughed, embarassed by what had occured. "Thank you very much, it's no problem",
I said, and laughed as well, before she got the next order.
