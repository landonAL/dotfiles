One of the potential jobs that seemed interesting was maintaining 5G towers, for it is not reliant on the sustainability of AI and provides genuine benefits for essentially everyone. In this field, even if AI fails and stops being used, the job would only be slightly more laborious and would probably be cheaper for the owner because he does not have to maintain advanced AI systems. Moreover, 5G is a massive improvement over 4G and benefits everything from cloud gaming to AI infrastructure, video analytics, and healthcare. 5G is the next logical step in cellular network development, and they will require people to maintain them.
