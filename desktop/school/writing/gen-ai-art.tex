\title{Generative AI Art}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\maketitle
Generative AI art can not surpass human art in terms of value. Although AI art may appear equal to or even surpass human art in face-level quality, regardless of how infrequently it does appear to be better, the true value of art comes primarily from the thought, intention, and effort behind it. AI is not alive, and therefore has no critical thinking or emotions, leading every ``decision'' that it makes while generating art to be a pale and lifeless imitation of genuine human creativity, experience, and talent; however, most AI art tends to be disfigured nonsense, or something that is obviously fake.
