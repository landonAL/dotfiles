A lie to a recipient is a false or purposefully ambiguous statement given by a living being, who is aware
that their statement is false. However, one can lie to himself by reaffirming a belief to himself that he
knows is false, whether by a conscious effort or by surrounding himself with other people who believe the
false idea, thereby subconsciously clouding his logical judgement often with emotion.
