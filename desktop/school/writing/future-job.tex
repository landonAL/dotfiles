In the future, I would like to get a master's degree or PhD in computer science and work in that field. There are many job opportunities in that field, so I do not yet know where I would go, but I would like to work at a FAANG company if I was able to stay close to home; however, many modern technology companies are incorporating generative AI into the developer's work and are even basing their job efficiency on how much they use AI, which is terrible for the developer and the user of the product. If generative AI continues to be forced onto developers during their jobs, I would rather go into a different field. I do not know what other fields I would be interested in doing, but I am a fast learner and enjoy learning, so I know that I am able to go into any field quite quickly if I desired it.
