\maketitle

On January 3, 2026, U.S. president Donald Trump ordered a military attack into Venezuela to capture Venezuelan president Nicolas Maduro and his wife, reportedly because of drug and weapons charges. Multiple media outlets asked Secretary of State Marco Rubio if the U.S. was running Venezuela, to which he slightly avoided the question and stated that the U.S. is directing the future of the country. He was also asked if he believes that the Venezuelan Interim President Delcy Rodriguez is now the legitimate president of Venezuela, however he responded that ``this is not about the legitimate president.'' Venezuelan Defense Minister Vladimir Patrino said on national television that their army backed Vice President Delcy Rodriguez to act as interim president. Maduro is being charged with narco-terrorism, conspiring to traffic cocaine into the U.S., and using machine guns to carry out the conspiracy. However, Trump made it clear that Venezuela's oil reserves, the largest proven reserve in the world at approximately 17 percent of the global total, was a core motivation for the attack.
